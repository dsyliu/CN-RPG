# 8-Chapter Story Outline

Companion document to `DESIGN.md`. One paragraph per chapter, plus scene breakdown and
生字 placement. **For review before any code is written** — Chapter 1's tone and mechanics
should be set by where the story ends up, not by what is convenient in week one.

Lesson order is fixed by the textbook and is never rearranged.

---

## The frame

You are new at Chinese school and you cannot read a word. On your first day you are told
your friend is waiting for you — at the school on the far side of the year. So you walk the
year to get there.

That is the whole premise, and it is deliberately thin. It gives the game one question
(Lesson 1's own: **我的朋友在哪裡**), one direction (forward, through autumn to spring),
and one reason to collect people. The answer arrives in Lesson 16, when 明明 introduces
himself and you finally have the words to introduce everyone you gathered on the road.

**The clock.** The story runs one US school year, September to May, and the textbook
supplies the seasons: harvest fruit in Lesson 2, the 圓月 of Lesson 5, rain in Lessons 7–8,
猴子's 新衣 at 春節 in Lesson 10, 雪 in Lesson 12, spring and a schoolyard in Lesson 16.

**The party.** Everyone is from the textbook. 小小, 小石, 中中, 明明 (言立明), 林友友,
江禾中 — plus the animals: the 小松鼠 you counted in Chapter 1, the 小豬 you draw in
Chapter 2, the 猴子 from Lesson 10, the 小狗 from Lesson 16's story, and the 雪人, who
joins in Chapter 6 and does not last.

## Chapter 1 — 少了一頭驢

**單元 1 · Lessons 1–2 · 細心 · September, the mountain road**

You meet 小小 at the foot of a mountain, driving **十** donkeys to market, and he lets you
walk with him because he cannot count and watch the road at the same time. The mountain is
Lesson 1's mountain — you go **上山找老虎**, and the 老虎 is real but not dangerous; it
wants to be counted too. On the far side is the orchard from Lesson 2, **一棵樹上十樣果**,
and the keeper will not hand over fruit to a stranger who cannot **握握手** and count what
they are owed. Then, on the road home, 小小 gets tired, climbs onto a donkey, counts again,
and there are only **九**. He panics. You look carefully, and you can see what he cannot:
he is sitting on the tenth one. **細心** — look properly before you panic. He joins you,
and he is the first answer to the question you started with.

- **Scene 1 — the mountain path.** Count 小松鼠 hiding from the 老虎: 一二三四五. The 老虎
  challenges you to a counting contest rather than a fight. 加減法 as the mechanic
  (一加三等於四).
- **Scene 2 — the orchard.** Ten fruits on one tree: 六七八九十. Greet the keeper —
  握握手, 點點頭 — and count the load by hundreds and thousands: 百, 千.
- **Scene 3 — the missing donkey.** All twelve characters in review. The 九/十 distinction
  *is* the plot.

**生字:** S1 一二三四五 · S2 六七八九十百千 · S3 all, with 九/十 load-bearing
**Mechanic introduced:** answer-to-attack, 加減法
**Companion:** 小小 (and the 小松鼠 follows at a distance)

## Chapter 2 — 氣球飛上天

**單元 2 · Lessons 3–4 · 作夢 · October, the schoolyard and the sky**

The road passes a schoolyard where 老師 is leading **拍拍手** — big hands and small hands,
three claps up, three claps down, one hard clap in the middle — and you cannot join in
because you cannot follow the instructions. Learning them is how you get in. There you meet
小石, who has bought an enormous balloon, and the balloon takes him. You go up after him,
and steering in the sky means knowing **左** from **右** and **上** from **下** with nothing
to reference but your own two hands — **五個在左、五個在右**. The 月 has **尖** teeth and
bites the balloon, and 小石 starts to fall. There is no time to climb down, so you draw
something soft to catch him, following the chant from Lesson 4 exactly:
胖胖身體像冬瓜，尖尖耳朵「工」嘴巴. The 小豬 you draw is real enough to land on. Then you
wake up on the grass beside the schoolyard, and so does 小石, and neither of you is sure
which part was the dream. **作夢** — a bad dream is only a dream. The pig drawing is still
in your pocket.

- **Scene 1 — the schoolyard.** 拍拍手 as a rhythm game: 上 拍三下, 下 拍三下, 中間 用力
  拍一下. 老師的大手, 小朋友的小手. All seven of Lesson 3 live in this one chant.
- **Scene 2 — the sky.** Navigate by 左/右 using your own ten fingers. 有 and 不 as the
  gates (會寫字、會拍球，就是**不**開口).
- **Scene 3 — drawing the pig.** The 工 mouth and the 尖 ears are literal instructions —
  draw wrong and you get a wrong pig. Then the fall, the landing, and waking up.

**生字:** S1 上中下大小手力 · S2 左右有不 · S3 工口尖
**Mechanic introduced:** instruction-following (直接畫), directions
**Companions:** 小石, the 小豬

## Chapter 3 — 森林大火

**單元 3 · Lessons 5–6 · 保護環境 · November, the night mountain and the forest**

Night, and the way through is Lesson 5's landscape: 山頂尖尖, 土路彎彎, 森林, 方屋, 圓月.
You cannot read the road, but you can read its *shapes* — and this is the chapter where the
game shows you that the characters **are** the shapes. 山 is a mountain. 火 is a fire. 水 is
water. 木 is a tree, and 林 is two of them. Then the forest catches: **啊！火！大火！
山上大火！下山！** and you are running down with the animals, and at the bottom there is
**大水**, and you are running back up. Everything the 小松鼠 had is gone. In the morning the
animals decide to plant, and 烏雲 and 太陽 and 風 all come to help, and one tree at a time
**木** becomes **林** again. The last of it is finished on **媽媽的生日**, and what you have
to give her is a **卡**片 with a **紅** flower on it and a forest behind it.
**保護環境** — you do not get the forest back quickly.

- **Scene 1 — the night mountain.** Navigate by shape: 尖, 彎, 方, 圓. The **象形**
  mechanic is introduced here — the pictograph morphs into the character.
- **Scene 2 — 火 and 水.** Up and down the mountain between fire and flood, Lesson 5's
  chant as the level structure. 象形 at full tilt.
- **Scene 3 — planting, and a birthday.** 木 → 林 over many small actions. 天, 早, 生日,
  卡, 也, 紅.

**生字:** S1 山 月 土 木 林 · S2 火 水 · S3 木 林 天 生 日 卡 早 也 紅
**Mechanic introduced:** 象形 pictograph morphing (山 火 水 木 日 月 土 — curriculum
objective L5.7)
**Companion:** the 小松鼠, who has nowhere else to go

## Chapter 4 — 下雨天真好玩兒

**單元 4 · Lessons 7–8 · 樂觀 · December, the hillside and a window**

The hillside is covered in **紅花** and **白花**, and the only way past the gatekeeper is to
say which there are **多** of and which there are **少** of — Lesson 7's whole comparison,
as a puzzle. Then **烏雲來了，下雨了**, and the rain falls on the 花 and the 樹 and the 草,
and you end up indoors with 中中, who is bored out of his mind. **大風，大雨，快走開** —
he wants to go to the 公園 to 踢足球, he wants to go to the 海邊 to 玩沙, and instead it is
grey and he is stuck. So you draw on the fogged window with a finger, and the characters
you have learned appear in the mist, and it turns out to be the best afternoon either of you
has had. You settle every argument about what to draw next by **猜拳** — 剪刀、石頭、布 —
and when the sun finally comes out you go and 玩水 on the wet 草地 anyway. **樂觀** — the
rainy day was the good day.

- **Scene 1 — the flower hillside.** 多/少 comparison, 的 as the connector, 雲 and 雨
  arriving. Lesson 7's 句型: ..有..也有.., 很多, 很少, 多少.
- **Scene 2 — the window.** Finger-writing in the fog. This is the one place the game lets
  a child *write* a character, and it is never graded — it is a toy, which is exactly what
  the story says it is. 我想去… as the wishing pattern.
- **Scene 3 — 猜拳, then outside.** 剪刀石頭布 with 中中 as a real mechanic with real luck.
  石, 足球, 沙.

**生字:** S1 雲 花 雨 白 多 少 的 · S2 風 明 子 玩 · S3 石 沙 足 玩
**Mechanic introduced:** 猜拳; fog-writing (ungraded)
**Companion:** 中中

## Chapter 5 — 紅雞媽媽

**單元 5 · Lessons 9–10 · 幫忙 · Late January, the rice field and 春節**

A grain of rice stops you on the path and introduces itself: **我是一粒小白米**, and it has
nothing much to say for itself except that a 農夫 worked in the **田** in the sun to make
it, and you should not leave it on your plate. It takes you to 紅雞媽媽, who is putting
seeds in the **土** with all her chicks, and the 大白鵝 and the 小花貓 who would rather play
than help. This is the chapter where you start **building** characters instead of just
recognising them — 月 plus 土 makes a **肚**, 日 plus 生 makes a **星**, two 人 on the 土
make **坐** — and everything you build, the farm needs. By the time the rice comes in it is
**春節**, and the **猴子** has a new coat for every day of the week, and there is 烤肉 and
唱歌 and 跳舞. Then the goose and the cat turn up wanting rice. 紅雞媽媽 asks how they dare.
You are the one who decides, and **幫忙** cuts both ways: they should have helped, and you
should feed them anyway.

- **Scene 1 — the rice field.** 一粒米's monologue in the first person. 人, 米, 田, 是, 我.
  The 農夫 and 辛苦.
- **Scene 2 — the farm, and 拼字.** The composition mechanic: 月＋土＝肚, 日＋生＝星,
  人＋人＋土＝坐 (curriculum objective L10.6). The goose and cat refuse; 沒 and 來.
- **Scene 3 — 春節.** 星期一 to 星期日 with the 猴子's new clothes. 在, 去, 和, 肉. The
  decision about the goose and the cat.

**生字:** S1 人 來 米 田 是 我 · S2 沒 肚 星 坐 · S3 在 去 和 肉
**Mechanic introduced:** 拼字 character composition
**Companion:** the 猴子, and the 一粒米 rides in your pocket

## Chapter 6 — 眉毛搬家

**單元 6 · Lessons 11–12 · 說謝謝 · February, a giant face and the snow**

You wake up small, on a face the size of a hill, and the **五官** are arguing. The 目 can
see, the 耳 can hear, the 舌 can 舔, the 牙 can 咬, the 口 can talk — and the 眉毛 sits
above them all and cannot do anything at all, so they vote to make it move. It tries the
chin. It tries beside the nose. Nothing fits, everything looks wrong, and everyone is
pleased with themselves. Then it begins to **雪**, and snow melts on a warm forehead, and
the water runs straight down into the **目**, and there is nothing in the way. Nobody says
sorry, but somebody says **謝謝**, and the 眉毛 goes home. Out on the 雪地 the 小狗 and the
小雞 are walking about printing **梅花** and **竹葉** with their feet, and you build a
**雪人** out of the snow, and he comes with you for a while. He has no 手 and no 腳 and he
cannot run. When the 太陽 comes out you both know what is going to happen, and you say
謝謝 to him too, before you can't. **說謝謝** — say it while there is someone to say it to.

- **Scene 1 — the face.** One puzzle per sense: 目 sees what you missed, 耳 hears what is
  hidden, 舌 tastes, 牙 bites through, 心 is how you know. The 眉毛 is mocked.
- **Scene 2 — the eyebrow moves.** Try it in three wrong places. Then the snow starts and
  the reason becomes obvious. 好 and 正 arrive through 口說好話、做好事、走正路.
- **Scene 3 — the 雪地.** 小狗 and 小雞 print 梅花 and 竹葉. Build the 雪人. 看 and 見.
  Then 太陽 一出雪就消.

**生字:** S1 目 耳 舌 牙 心 · S2 好 正 看 見 · S3 雪 地 竹 你 他 看 見
**Mechanic introduced:** five senses as distinct puzzle types; weather reading
**Companion:** the 雪人, briefly. This is the chapter that is allowed to be sad.

## Chapter 7 — 我的手拿不出來了

**單元 7 · Lessons 13–14 · 安全 · March, a mirror and a market**

The thaw, and a **鏡子** in the road that will not let you past until you match it. It does
what you do: 我笑你也笑，我哭你也哭 — and the way through is Lesson 13's whole vocabulary
of faces, 哭 like a 喇叭, 笑 like a flower, 叫 like a 鴨, 唱 like a 青蛙. Past the mirror is
the market, and the market only trades in **量詞**: nobody will sell you 李子 unless you ask
for **一籃**, and there is 一條麵包 and 一塊糕 and 一串葡萄 and 一根草 and 一把刀 and
一頭大象 and 一隻貓, and getting the counter wrong gets you the wrong thing, sometimes
comically. Then you find 小小 at a table with his fist in a jar, crying, because he grabbed
as many **花生米** as his hand could hold and now his hand will not come out. Everyone tries
pulling. What works is **少拿一點兒**. **安全** — the grab is what trapped him.

- **Scene 1 — the mirror.** It copies your answers, so you have to be right twice, or right
  in reverse. 哭, 笑, 叫, 唱, 巴, 了.
- **Scene 2 — the 量詞 market.** Pair number + 量詞 + noun to buy anything (curriculum
  objective L14.4). 比 and 比一比 to choose between two of everything.
- **Scene 3 — the peanut jar.** 小小's hand. 走, 了, and the realisation that less is how
  you get out.

**生字:** S1 哭 笑 叫 唱 巴 了 · S2 比 李 刀 位 草 象 呀 · S3 走 了, all in review
**Mechanic introduced:** 量詞 word-building; the mirror (answer-mirroring)
**Companion:** none new — this is 小小's chapter, and he has been with you since Chapter 1

## Chapter 8 — 小狗的早飯

**單元 8 · Lessons 15–16 · 愛護動物 · April, home and the school**

Almost there, and the question from the first day comes back as an actual question:
**什麼是朋友?** The answer in Lesson 15 is plain — 朋友就是你喜歡的人，也是喜歡你的人 —
and it explicitly includes 一隻貓、一隻狗、或是一隻小老鼠, which is lucky, because look at
who you are travelling with. You sort them all out by what they are made of: the ones with
人 in them, the ones with 水, the ones with 草, the ones with 肉. Then the last night before
the school, and there is a 小狗 who needs feeding every morning, and you are in a hurry, and
you forget. You are at 學校 all day. The dog is not. **愛護動物** — being loved by something
means it is depending on you. And then the schoolyard in spring, and a boy walks up and
says **我姓言，我叫言立明，大家都叫我明明**, and introduces **林友友** and **江禾中**, and
then looks at you and waits. So you say your own name — the one you chose at the very
beginning — and then you introduce 小小, and 小石, and 中中, and the 小松鼠, and the 小豬,
and the 猴子, and the 小狗, one at a time, in Chinese, because by now you can.
**我的朋友在哪裡.** They are all standing right there.

- **Scene 1 — 什麼是朋友.** The 部件 sorting mechanic: group everyone you have met by
  radical — 人, 水, 草, 肉 (curriculum objective L15.7). 交朋友, 朋, 友, 隻, 女, 男.
- **Scene 2 — the dog's breakfast.** 學校 all day; the dog waiting. Short, quiet, no puzzle
  at the end of it.
- **Scene 3 — the schoolyard.** 明明's introduction, then yours, then everyone's. 姓, 言,
  立, 江, 禾, 這, 那 — and 這是 / 那是 as the pattern that carries the whole ending.

**生字:** S1 交 朋 友 隻 女 男 · S2 校 隻 · S3 姓 言 立 江 禾 這 那
**Mechanic introduced:** 部件 radical sorting — which is also the 字典 screen
**Companions:** 明明, 林友友, 江禾中, the 小狗 — and the party is complete

## Notes for review

**Three things I want checked before this gets built:**

1. **Chapter 6 is the sad one.** The 眉毛 is humiliated and the 雪人 melts, both in the same
   chapter, and I have put them next to each other on purpose — the eyebrow teaches you to
   say thank you in time, and then the snowman gives you one chance to actually do it. If
   that is too much for a 6-year-old, the fix is to keep the melt and soften the mocking.

2. **Chapter 8, Scene 2 has no puzzle.** The dog waits all day and you were not there, and
   then the scene ends. I think the game needs one moment that is not a game. But it is the
   one place a child might click through expecting something to do, so say if you would
   rather it earned its place mechanically.

3. **Chapter 5 asks a child to make a moral call** — whether to feed the goose and the cat
   who would not help. The textbook's 紅雞媽媽 says no. I have written it so the player says
   yes, which is arguably me overriding the curriculum's own story. Worth a teacher's
   opinion; it is a one-line change either way.

**One revision to `DESIGN.md`:** the companion column there lists only four chapters as
granting a party member. This outline gives one to every chapter except 7 — 小小, then 小石
and the 小豬, the 小松鼠, 中中, the 猴子, the 雪人, and finally 明明/友友/中中/小狗. The
design doc should be updated to match once this outline is approved.
