Two kinds of question. A world question's answer is whatever is on screen — count your own donkeys. A scheduler question's target comes from mastery: lowest first, with a known character folded back in every fourth turn. Choices go 2 below mastery 2, then 3, then 4. Distractors come from the far pool while a character is new and the near pool once it is known — 十/千 and 六/八 are near, 九/五 are far. Mastery rises 1 for an unaided correct answer, not at all when a hint was used (gold bar), and falls 1 on a miss. There is no losing.

Arrow keys or WASD to walk. SPACE to talk to whoever is blocking the road. Get 小小's ten donkeys over the mountain, through the orchard, and home.
